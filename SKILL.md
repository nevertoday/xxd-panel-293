## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design is one poetic stamp, not a mascot and not a commercial logo. Use simple rounded shapes, flat color and a hand contour. A tiny expression or a small awkward action may appear, without turning the subject into a new character. Paper grain, gouache and risograph misregistration stay visible. Color is warm ivory, mist blue, sage, coral or cream, taken from the photograph. One symbol only, in a large field. Type is a tiny editorial note. Reject realism, smooth vector, 3D, a template icon and cute overload.

These overlay rules add to `references/soldier-runtime.md`. They never replace the source brief or the family runtime contract.
